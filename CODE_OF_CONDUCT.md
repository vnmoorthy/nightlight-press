# Code of Conduct

Nightlight Press makes books for small children, and we want the people building it to treat each other with the same care. This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text there is the one that applies; this page is a summary.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, and for the children who read these books

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Sharing any real child's personal details, photos or stories in issues, pull requests or test data
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [vnmoorthy](https://github.com/vnmoorthy), through GitHub. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
