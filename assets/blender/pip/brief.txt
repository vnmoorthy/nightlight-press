Write ONE complete, self-contained Blender 5.2.1 Python (bpy) script, run headless as
  Blender -b --factory-startup -P pip_scene.py --python-exit-code 1
that builds and renders "Pip", the PlayRobot mascot. Keep it compact (aim for under 350 lines), robust and simple: a solid simple result beats an ambitious broken one. No external files/addons/images.

PARAMETERS (environment variables):
- PIP_MODE: "anim" (default) or "hero".
- PIP_OUT: anim -> output path prefix for PNG frames, e.g. /abs/frames/pip_ (Blender appends ####); hero -> full .png path.
- PIP_RES: square resolution (anim default 1080, hero default 1600).
- PIP_SAMPLES: EEVEE render samples (default 16).
- PIP_FRAME_START / PIP_FRAME_END: anim frame range to render (defaults 1 and 150).
- PIP_FRAME: frame used for hero mode (default 38).

ANIMATION: 150 frames at 30 fps (5.0 s), frames 1..150, and it must loop SEAMLESSLY: frame 151 would equal frame 1. Drive every motion with periodic functions of phase p = (frame-1)/150 (sin/cos of 2*pi*k*p, integer k) and insert a keyframe on EVERY frame 1..150 with LINEAR interpolation (set interpolation on the keyframe points directly; in Blender 5.x do not rely on action.fcurves existing - iterate keyframes safely, e.g. via obj.animation_data.action and try/except, or just set bpy.context.preferences.edit.keyframe_new_interpolation_type = 'LINEAR' before inserting keys). Motions:
- Whole Pip (parent empty) gently bobs up/down (amplitude ~4% of Pip height, k=2) and yaws side to side +/-15 degrees (30 degree total swing, k=1) around the vertical axis.
- Pip's RIGHT arm (screen-left side is fine, whichever reads better) waves: raised up and rocking +/-25 degrees around its shoulder pivot, k=3. Left arm hangs relaxed with a tiny sway.
- Antenna star gently spins/pulses (scale 1.0 +/- 0.08, k=2).
- 8-10 small gold sparkles (tiny emissive 4-point stars or icospheres, gold, emission ~8) drift slowly upward behind/around Pip; each one's height = base + span*((p + offset_i) % 1.0) and its scale goes to 0 near both ends of its travel (e.g. scale = s*sin(pi*((p+offset_i)%1.0))) so the wrap is invisible and the loop is seamless.

PIP'S FIXED LOOK (all colours are sRGB hex - convert to linear for Base Color: c<=0.04045 ? c/12.92 : ((c+0.055)/1.055)**2.4):
- A small round toy robot, cute and chunky, ~1.2 units tall overall, standing on the origin, facing the camera (camera is on the -Y axis looking toward +Y, so all face features sit on the -Y side of the head).
- Body: a slightly squashed sphere (smooth shaded, subdivision or UV sphere with enough segments), glossy sky-blue #6FC3FF (roughness ~0.25, a bit of coat if available: "Coat Weight").
- Head: a round cream-white #FFF7E8 sphere sitting on top of the body (head ~ as wide as the body or slightly smaller), roughness ~0.45, soft clay/toy look.
- Eyes: two friendly dark-navy (#14214D) vertical OVAL eyes (scaled spheres) on the front of the head, slightly embedded so they read clearly, each with a tiny white emissive highlight dot.
- Mouth: a small smile - a partial torus arc (bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_torus_add then delete the upper half of verts with bmesh, or a curve bevel arc) in dark navy below the eyes on the head front.
- Antenna: short thin cylinder on top of the head, topped with a 5-point STAR (build with bmesh: 10 alternating outer/inner verts, extruded to a little thickness) in glowing gold #FFD36E, emission strength ~6-10 so it blooms. Add a small gold point light at the star (energy ~20 W, warm) so it casts a glow on the head.
- Two little dark (#1C2238) wheels: short wide cylinders or tori at the lower left and right of the body, axis along X, partly tucked under the body.
- Two small rounded arms: capsule-like (scaled sphere or cylinder with round ends) in sky-blue, attached at the body sides; each arm parented to its own shoulder empty for rotation.
- Soft clay/toy materials, smooth shading everywhere. No text.

SCENE / LOOK:
- Background: deep navy #0B1A4A world colour (world strength ~1). Behind Pip, a large vertical backdrop plane (facing camera, a few units behind) with an EMISSION shader using a spherical/radial gradient: soft royal-blue #1F3FA6 glow centred behind Pip fading smoothly to the navy #0B1A4A at the edges (Texture Coordinate Object/Generated -> Gradient Texture SPHERICAL or a distance-based ramp -> ColorRamp -> Emission strength 1.0). The colour at the plane edges must match the world navy so there are no visible edges. Use view transform 'Standard' so the hex colours read true.
- Lighting: soft large key area light from front-upper-left, a cool blue rim light from behind-right, soft fill. Pip must be well lit and clearly readable against the dark background.
- Camera: perspective ~65-85 mm lens, at about eye level slightly above, on -Y, aimed at Pip's centre; Pip fills ~60-65% of the frame height and stays fully in frame during the whole bob/sway/wave (including antenna star and waving arm). Gentle DOF focused on Pip (f-stop ~4 so Pip is sharp; sparkles may soften).
- Bloom: Blender 5.x compositor glare exactly like the reference below (standalone CompositorNodeTree group with group output socket assigned to scene.compositing_node_group; glare Type 'Bloom' or 'Fog Glow', threshold ~1.0, strength ~0.8).
- EEVEE engine as in the reference (try 'BLENDER_EEVEE', fall back to 'BLENDER_EEVEE_NEXT'), scene.eevee.taa_render_samples = PIP_SAMPLES.

MODES:
- anim: film_transparent False, PNG RGB, filepath = PIP_OUT, frame range from env, bpy.ops.render.render(animation=True).
- hero: film_transparent True, PNG RGBA, hide the backdrop plane from render (hide_render=True), keep Pip, star glow and a few sparkles, render frame PIP_FRAME at PIP_RES, write_still=True.
- At the end print one summary line with mode, resolution, samples, frames and output path.

def material(name, color, roughness=0.66, emission=0.0,
             grain=False, emission_color=None):
    mat = bpy.data.materials.new(name)
    mat.use_nodes = True
    bs = mat.node_tree.nodes.get("Principled BSDF")
    bs.inputs["Base Color"].default_value = (*color, 1)
    bs.inputs["Roughness"].default_value = roughness
    bs.inputs["Specular IOR Level"].default_value = 0.3
    bs.inputs["Sheen Weight"].default_value = 0.0
    if emission:
        bs.inputs["Emission Color"].default_value = (
            *(emission_color if emission_color is not None else color), 1)
        bs.inputs["Emission Strength"].default_value = emission
    if grain:
        noise = mat.node_tree.nodes.new("ShaderNodeTexNoise")
        noise.inputs["Scale"].default_value = 100
        noise.inputs["Detail"].default_value = 2
        bump = mat.node_tree.nodes.new("ShaderNodeBump")
        bump.inputs["Strength"].default_value = 0.04
        bump.inputs["Distance"].default_value = 0.015
        mat.node_tree.links.new(noise.outputs["Fac"], bump.inputs["Height"])
        mat.node_tree.links.new(bump.outputs["Normal"], bs.inputs["Normal"])
    mat.diffuse_color = (*color, 1)
    return mat
