Great - that script ran first time in Blender 5.2.1. I rendered test frames 1, 60, 120 and 180 at 480x270. Art-direction notes for a revised version (keep everything that works, same env-var interface, same structure):

1. TOO DIM / GREY: the paper pages read as flat grey and the whole image is a bit muddy. The shot must feel bright, sunny and warm like a storybook morning. Raise the overall exposure (e.g. view exposure +0.4..+0.7, or stronger key/sun), make the paper read as warm cream FFF8EC (not grey), and lift the shadows with a warmer, stronger world/fill. Keep the AgX transform but avoid a washed-out look (a slightly punchier look such as 'AgX - Medium High Contrast' or 'AgX - Punchy' if that look name exists; set it defensively).
2. MAGIC GLOW: the golden light coming out of the pages is too subtle. From about frame 85 on, the gutter should clearly glow warm golden (stronger golden point light, plus a soft emissive golden glow volume/plane over the gutter with a soft falloff - e.g. a few stacked semi-transparent emissive discs or a low-alpha emissive plane - that blooms). Make the glare/bloom a bit stronger (lower threshold ~1.0, a little more strength/size) so the sparkles, stars and glow visibly bloom - but don't blow out the pages.
3. SPARKLES / STARS: make them a little larger and more numerous near the gutter (keep ~90 sparkles, ~18 stars), and make the paper stars bigger (about 0.07-0.11 across) so they read clearly as stars at 1600x900. Start the first sparkles slightly earlier (~frame 70) and keep a steady stream until the end so the final frames are full of rising glowing motes.
4. PAGE TURN: the flipping page looks like a stiff flat board - make the paper curl obvious (bend angle around 50-70 degrees mid-flip, page subdivided enough to curve smoothly), with the bend direction so the free edge trails behind like a real page turn.
5. FRAMING: at the end (frames 150-200) the open spread sits a bit low and close to the bottom edge. Center the open spread vertically with a bit more space below it and room above for the rising sparkles. Keep the slow push-in. The far background currently only shows the table; tilt/position so a hint of the warm cream backdrop and the soft blurred props (mug, plant, ball) remain visible at the top of frame, slightly larger, softly blurred (use f-stop ~2.0).
6. WOOD: the table planks are a little uniform and orange; vary plank tones more (honey, lighter oak, slightly darker caramel) and keep them rounded and matte.
7. Remove the use_nodes deprecation warnings only if trivial (not required).
